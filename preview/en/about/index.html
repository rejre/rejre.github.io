What is Fengwuzhi?Our Story

Fengwuzhi is one person's record of a life. Days pass quickly, and little is worth keeping — the way to make a bowl of soup, the way to arrange a room, the way to walk a mountain path. Pick those things out, write them down, add photographs, and gather them slowly: that is this journal.

Nothing here chases trends. Updates follow the seasons: when a solar term arrives, so does an article. Food follows the weather, the home follows warm and cold, footsteps follow the colour of the hills — the rhythm of recording is simply the rhythm of days.

Why solar terms?Solar Terms

The twenty-four solar terms are China's old scale for arranging life: when to add a layer, when to stew a soup, when to go out and see the mountains — the ancestors wrote it all into the terms. Fengwuzhi has moved that scale into a website: the paper colour, the accent, the opening scene and the seasonal signs all switch automatically to the date on which you open the page.

Whichever day you come, that day's Fengwuzhi is what you see. Tap the term tag at the lower left of the homepage to open the Solar-Term Drawer and preview any term's skin and signs; the three pentads of each term follow the classical tradition of the Yizhoushu (Explanations of the Seasons), unaltered.
